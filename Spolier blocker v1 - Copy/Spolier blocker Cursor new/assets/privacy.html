Privacy Policy (local)

Spoiler Shield stores the following data locally in your browser using chrome.storage:

  • Custom keywords — keywords you add to identify spoilers.
  • False positives — tokens you mark as not spoilers.
  • Settings — per-site controls and toggles.

We do not collect or transmit the pages you visit or their content by default. The extension may call third-party APIs you opt into (for example TMDB lookup) — those interactions are performed directly from your browser and third parties' terms apply. If you plan to send any page content to a remote server, disclose it here and obtain explicit consent from users.

Contact & Data Deletion

To delete stored data, open the extension options and clear custom keywords and settings, or uninstall the extension which removes local storage. For support, add a support URL in your Chrome Web Store listing.
