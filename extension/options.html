Backup and restore

Backups contain protection settings and keywords. Credentials and account data are never exported. Imports show a preview before replacing your protection settings.

What protection covers

Text, image descriptions and related thumbnails on ordinary HTTP/HTTPS pages, including new posts loaded as you scroll. Matching is heuristic: no blocker can guarantee complete spoiler protection.

No image-pixel recognition, video/audio transcription, shadow-root content support, or access to browser-internal pages. Strict mode may hide unrelated content with the same name. Site markup can change.
