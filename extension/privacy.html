Privacy and permissions

Version 2.0 · September 19, 2026

On-device protection

Page text, descriptions and local detection results stay in the page's isolated extension context. Spoiler Shield does not upload browsing history, page contents or analytics. Your watchlist, keywords and settings use local extension storage, not browser sync.

When upgrading under the same extension ID, supported legacy settings are copied from sync storage. Old stored account data and the old synced TMDB key are removed after migration. A supported TMDB key is moved to restricted local storage. The extension no longer provides Google sign-in.

Optional lookup

If you enable TMDB lookup, your search terms, requested title IDs and your TMDB credential are sent directly to api.themoviedb.org over HTTPS. TMDB also receives ordinary network metadata, including your IP address. No requests go to a shared proxy. Credentials are excluded from backups and never returned to content scripts. Disconnecting deletes the locally saved credential and removes the optional host permission.

Permissions

Website content access is required to inspect and conceal matching blocks on HTTP/HTTPS pages. Storage saves preferences. Context menus let you block selected text. activeTab lets the popup show controls for the site you open it on. Optional TMDB host access is requested only when you enable lookup.

No identity, scripting, broad optional host, download-history, or externally-connectable permissions are requested.

Optional account settings backup

A configured build can connect to the maintainer's account service after you grant its exact optional host permission. The service stores your email, password hash, tier and explicitly uploaded protection settings. The extension stores its session token in restricted local storage, separate from backups. Website content and TMDB credentials are never uploaded. Cloud settings are not end-to-end encrypted.

Upload and download are explicit actions. Download requires confirmation; concurrent cloud changes reject stale uploads. The account website supports downloading your cloud settings and deleting your account. Free/Premium/Max quotas apply only to custom keyword entries; title packs and aliases do not consume them. Offline clients use cached policy until reconnecting.

Your data

Export and import settings from the options page. Removing the extension removes its local storage. Exported backups contain your chosen interests; store and share them accordingly. Legacy sync settings other than account/key fields are retained for recovery. This release does not erase copies held by your browser's sync service or exported backups.

Protection is a reading aid, not access control: website scripts and developer tools can still access the original page. Detection cannot inspect image pixels, audio, video frames, shadow-root content or browser-internal pages.
