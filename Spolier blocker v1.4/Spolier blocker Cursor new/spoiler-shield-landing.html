How it works

Three steps, zero effort

Install once. Add what you want to protect. Forget about spoilers forever.

1

Add your topic

Search movies or shows via TMDB for instant full setup — cast, characters, alternate titles included. Or type any keyword manually: a team name, a book, an event. Anything works.

2

Browse normally

Every page you visit is scanned in real time. Matching text and images are blurred before your eyes reach them — Reddit, Twitter, YouTube, Wikipedia, and everywhere else.

3

Reveal when you're ready

Hover or click any blur to see what's underneath. The extension learns from what you reveal and what you skip, quietly getting smarter over time with no manual adjustments needed.

Features

Not a basic keyword blocker

A full adaptive spoiler protection system — smart, fast, and private by design.

🧠

Learns from your behavior

A reinforcement learning system tracks what you reveal and what you flag as wrong. It reduces false positives automatically over time — no manual tuning, ever.

Unique to Spoiler Shield
🖼️

Blurs images too

Checks image alt text, captions, filenames, and CSS background images. A thumbnail named "lakers-finals-winner.jpg" gets caught even without any visible text near it.

⚡

Built for performance

Batched regex processing, MutationObserver pipeline, and smart throttling keep page load overhead under 320ms — completely invisible even on heavy Reddit or Wikipedia pages.

🔄

Auto-reblur timer

Peeked at something by accident? Configure a timer and the blur comes back automatically after a few seconds. Keeps your spoiler protection intact without any effort.

Unique to Spoiler Shield
🌐

Per-site control

Enable protection on Reddit and Twitter, disable it on sites you trust. Rules sync across all your devices and are remembered per domain automatically.

🎛️

Adjustable aggressiveness

Choose how many related phrases to block per title — from just the name, up to full cast, crew, and character lists. Four levels let you find the right balance.

🔁

Cross-device sync

Sign in with Google and your full setup — blocklist, custom keywords, learned weights, per-site settings — syncs automatically across every Chrome install you use.

🎨

Customisable blur styles

Choose between Gaussian blur, pixelate, or solid overlay. Adjust blur radius, reveal behaviour (hover, click, or double-click), and reblur timer to match your preference.

Privacy

Built with privacy first

Your data stays in your browser. We don't want it and we don't collect it.

🔒

No data leaves your device

Your watchlist, keywords, and learned weights are stored in your browser only. Never on our servers.

🚫

Zero tracking

No analytics, no usage tracking, no ad targeting. We don't know what you block or what you watch.

🔑

Secure API proxy

TMDB title lookups go through a rate-limited proxy. Your IP and search terms are never logged.

📄

Open about permissions

We only request the permissions we actually use. Every permission is explained clearly in our privacy policy.
