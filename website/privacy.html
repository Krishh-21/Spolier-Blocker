Your privacy,
in plain English.

Local by default

The extension scans page text and metadata on your device. The website includes no analytics, ad pixels or external font requests. Browsing content is not sent to the account service.

Optional accounts

Registration stores your email, a salted password hash, role and plan. A random session token keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. The server stores a hash of that token. Website cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict; HTTPS deployments also use Secure. The extension keeps its session in restricted local storage.

Settings backup

Uploading sends your selected titles, aliases, custom keywords, allowed text and site preferences to your configured service. Downloading replaces local settings only after confirmation. API credentials and session tokens are excluded. Cloud settings are stored in the server database; this is not end-to-end encryption. Administrators maintain that database.

Deletion and retention

Your account page lets you download your cloud settings and delete your account, settings and sessions. Local extension settings and exported files remain on your devices. Deployment operators must publish their contact details, backup retention, hosting location and any server-log retention before inviting public users.

Payments and lookup

No payment processing is enabled in this release. Optional TMDB lookup is a separate direct connection from your extension to TMDB and requires your own credential. Its requests include ordinary network metadata.

Self-hosted release, September 2026. Email verification/recovery, production monitoring and a hosted billing integration are not configured.
