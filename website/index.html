BEFORE THE CREDITS ROLL

A few good
questions.

Will it catch every spoiler?

No. Matching is heuristic. Strict mode hides mentions of selected topics; Balanced tries to keep ordinary discussion visible. Initial content may appear before scanning finishes. Choose Solid mask for the strongest concealment.

Does it protect games and YouTube?

It protects matching browser text and metadata about games, including guides, posts and YouTube cards. You can turn off YouTube protection or only its previews. It cannot inspect native games, video pixels, audio or shadow-root content.

Do I need an account?

No. Local protection works without one. An optional account lets you explicitly upload and download your settings. Your page contents and API credentials are never included.

Where can I install it?

The current release is available from GitHub for unpacked installation in Chromium. A Chrome Web Store listing has not been published. Follow the repository’s installation instructions.

What about live sport and streaming?

Topic protection works on surrounding browser text today. Time-delayed event filtering, streaming chat adapters and live transcription are future work, not shipped features.
